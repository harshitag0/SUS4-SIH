# Multilingual chatbot (SMS + WhatsApp)
Languages: Marathi, Hindi, English (later Konkani, Kannada). Channels: WhatsApp Business API, SMS fallback for poor connectivity.

Use cases in priority order: (1) alerts from the scenario module, (2) bond status and sign link, (3) today's price vs forecast, (4) quality tips after a failed grade, (5) FAQs.

Design rules: short messages, voice-note input, numbers over charts, always offer "talk to consultant".
Status: spec only.
