# Data

## Supply–Demand engine (trained model)
Training data lives in `services/ml/data/`. **Today it is sample (synthetic) data** generated by `services/ml/scripts/make_sample_data.py` — realistic structure (growth per market, COVID dip, price elasticity, season shape, grade mix, Cyclone Tauktae 2021 / unseasonal rain 2023 supply shocks), not real figures. Every row has `source=sample`, and the app labels the model "trained on sample data".

To use real data, replace the three CSVs (same columns) and retrain:

| File | Columns | Real source |
|---|---|---|
| `exports_monthly.csv` | `year, month (3–6), destination, grade (A/B/C), tons, avg_price_usd_kg, source` | APEDA Agri Exchange, fresh mango (HS 080450) country-wise monthly exports; grade split from exporters / packhouse records |
| `supply_monthly.csv` | `year, month, destination, grade, tons, shock (0/1 bad-weather year), source` | FPO packhouse intake of export-grade fruit; mark cyclone / unseasonal-rain years with `shock=1` |
| `markets.csv` | `destination, indian_diaspora_pct, premium (0/1), air_hours, needs_treatment (0/1)` | census / MEA diaspora figures, airline schedules, importing-country phytosanitary rules |

Retrain: `POST http://localhost:8000/supply-demand/train` (or delete `services/ml/models/supply_demand.joblib` and restart the ML service). The model card in the app shows the new accuracy automatically.

## Price (cost) prediction engine (trained model)
`services/ml/data/prices_weekly.csv` — **sample** data from `scripts/make_price_data.py` (run after `make_sample_data.py`; it reads the Singapore demand-supply gap from those CSVs).

| Columns | Real source |
|---|---|
| `year, week (0 = Mar W4 … 11 = Jun W3), week_label, district, grade, price_inr_kg, arrivals_tons, market_arrivals_tons, sg_gap_pct, shock, source` | Agmarknet / APMC (Vashi, Ratnagiri, Kolhapur) weekly modal prices and arrivals for Alphonso; `sg_gap_pct` from the supply-demand data; `shock=1` for cyclone / unseasonal-rain years |

Retrain: `POST http://localhost:8000/price/train`. Post-harvest cost assumptions (packing, cold chain, docs, FPO fee, mandi commission) are constants at the top of `services/ml/app/modules/price/service.py`.

## Cost & Profit engine (trained model)
`services/ml/data/costs_yearly.csv` — **sample** data from `scripts/make_cost_data.py` (run after `make_price_data.py`).

| Columns | Real source |
|---|---|
| `year, district, labour, fertilizer, plant_protection, irrigation, bagging_materials, harvest_transport` (₹/acre), `yield_kg_acre` (marketable), `price_inr_kg`, `wage_index, diesel_inr_l, fertilizer_index, shock, source` | State agriculture dept / CACP cost-of-cultivation studies for Konkan mango, FPO member records; rural wage index (Labour Bureau), diesel retail price, fertilizer price index |

Retrain: `POST http://localhost:8000/cost/train`. Farm scaling assumptions (40 trees/acre, tree-age factor, irrigation adjustments, processing price) are constants at the top of `services/ml/app/modules/cost/service.py`. Farmers' own actual costs are saved in MongoDB (`users.costs`) and compared with the district average.

## Live data (no training needed)
- Weather: Open-Meteo per district (`server/src/lib/weather.js`).
- Platform demand: real buyer requirements in MongoDB (`/api/market/live`).
- Mandi prices: data.gov.in Agmarknet when `DATA_GOV_IN_API_KEY` is set.

## Still assumptions
- Export freight (₹20 + ₹13 per flight hour), treatment costs (USA irradiation ₹30/kg, Japan VHT ₹40/kg), target margin 20%: constants in `services/ml/app/modules/cost/service.py` — replace with real freight quotes from the FPO's exporter.
- Marketable yield per tree by tree age (5 / 20 / 35 / 40 kg): `server/src/modules/farmers/service.js`.
- USD→INR rate: `USD_INR` env (default 88).
